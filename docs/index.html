Sach - WhatsApp forward fact-checker ↗

Paste a viral WhatsApp forward and get a cited TRUE / FALSE / MISLEADING / UNVERIFIED verdict from PIB Fact Check and official sources, in English and 15 Indian languages, as a card ready to post back into the family group. Built solo, runs on free tiers, filed as submission #126.

AI agentsEvidence groundingFact-check APIsVercel
Problem

A viral forward reaches a family group in seconds; a human fact-check takes days. By then it has been believed, forwarded and acted on.

Architecture

A three-layer cascade: match the claim against PIB Fact Check's archive and published fact-checks first, then a live AI check grounded in official sources, and if the evidence is not there, an honest UNVERIFIED with a human tipline handoff - never a guess dressed as a verdict.

Outcome

Public 79-claim evaluation with zero overclaimed FALSE verdicts, abstention preferred over guessing. Live at sachcheck.in in English and 15 Indian languages, filed in the Code for a Billion judging window.

QuantPilot - research agent that shows its work ↗

        
An autonomous pre-market options research agent for US markets. It investigates 12 names across fragmented sources and produces trade cards where every number traces to the evidence packet it came from - including a verifier that catches the model misquoting its own data. Screening rank 252 of 547; demo score 12.5 of 15.

        
Live ExplorerGitHub repoBuild storyNext: Cluse ->

        
Multi-agent systemsCitation verificationPythonEval design

        
        

          
Problem
Research agents sound certain while misquoting their own data - and a trade idea built on a wrong number is worse than no idea at all.

          
Architecture
A multi-agent pipeline investigates 12 names across fragmented sources and assembles evidence packets. Every number on a trade card must trace back to its packet, and a separate verifier pass exists to catch the model misquoting its own evidence.

          
Outcome
Open Agent Hackathon 2026: screening rank 252 of 547, with a demo score of 12.5 of 15 - higher than all three Track 02 winners' demos (12.3 each).

Cluse - autonomous paper-trading desk ↗

        
A Nasdaq-100 options research desk running on virtual money: technical setups, an intraday news radar, market-regime filters, and strict risk rules with a drawdown kill-switch. The public page refreshes every 5 minutes in session, with an interactive 3D diagram of the whole system.

        
Live desk3D system diagramGitHub repoBack to Sach ->

        
Trading systemsRisk enginesDiscord botsThree.js

        
        

          
Problem
Strategy ideas are cheap. The hard part is a desk that sizes positions, takes profit, stops out and flattens by rule - proven without risking real capital.

          
Architecture
Nasdaq-100 technical setups pass through an intraday news radar and market-regime filters into a risk engine with conviction sizing, take-profit and stop-loss rules and a drawdown kill-switch. Everything flattens at the close; paper money only, by design.

          
Outcome
Runs in public as a live desk: refreshes every 5 minutes in session and republishes itself daily, with every position and exit on the page.

          
Proof
Live desk - 5-minute refresh in session - 0 real orders
